Program 06

Civil Service & Civic Engagement

Freedom is practiced, not just protected. CLF builds civic power through nonpartisan voter participation, civic education and a pipeline into public service, so our communities show up, are counted and have a seat in the rooms where decisions are made, from city hall to the White House.

Get supportVolunteer or partner

Rooted in the Legacy

In 1964, Malcolm X put the ballot at the center of the struggle in his speech “The Ballot or the Bullet.” An organized, voting community is a community that cannot be ignored.

What We Offer

Services

  • 01

    Voter registration & turnout

    Registration drives, reminders and rides to the polls at mosques, churches, campuses and barbershops.

  • 02

    Civic education

    Plain-language guides to the ballot, the issues and how local government works.

  • 03

    Public service pipeline

    Preparing community members to serve on boards and commissions, in government careers and in public office.

  • 04

    Convenings & forums

    Nonpartisan gatherings that put residents and elected leaders in the same room.

  • 05

    Restoration of rights

    Helping returning citizens understand and exercise their right to vote.

In the Room · Detroit

The Fierce Urgency of Now.

Turn Up & Turn Out the Vote: a night of community power and democracy in action, co-hosted by Community Life Forward and Dream of Detroit. Organizers, faith leaders, artists and elected officials came together around one message: show up, and bring your neighbors.

From the Archive

“Dedicated to Freedom, Justice and Equality”

That line ran on the masthead of Muhammad Speaks, the newspaper of the community that produced Malcolm X and Muhammad Ali. This 1974 front page called for justice for an unarmed Muslim man shot in Berkeley, for Black workers in Atlanta, and against the racist science of its day.

Fifty years later, Community Life Forward is built on the same three words, and answers the same cry with organizing, voter power and public service.

Peace Is Possible

Enough is enough.

Justice is made on the block: with young men who choose peace, elders who stand beside them and resource fairs that put help within reach of people coming home.

How It Works

Three steps forward.

  1. 01

    Register

    Make sure you and your household are registered at your current address.

  2. 02

    Learn

    Attend a civic education session or a community forum.

  3. 03

    Serve & organize

    Vote, bring three neighbors, and consider serving on a board or commission.

Who It's For

Eligibility

Every resident. CLF's civic work is strictly nonpartisan and does not support or oppose any candidate or party.
