SaafirRabb II

Saafir Rabb II is an entrepreneur and a creative driving force in impact-driven domestic and international enterprise. A proud son of Baltimore, Maryland, he is known for bringing structured thinking to complex work: clarifying vision, managing expectations and negotiating outcomes that work for everyone involved.

He leads InterCulture, Inc., which forms venture partnerships and structures investments for innovative, impact-oriented local and global enterprises. Its work spans corporate strategy, media, sports and entertainment advisory, philanthropic investment advisory and economic development, including impact investment partnerships, fund development, affordable housing and renewable energy initiatives in the U.S., Africa and the Middle East.

Baltimore, Maryland

Through 1RACE, a real estate venture partnership with IDSRES, he develops opportunistic and value-add affordable housing, logistics and mixed-use projects.

He founded and chairs Community Life Forward, a non-profit that builds academic, religious and community partnerships for meaningful impact across culturally diverse settings. Having grown up in and been mentored within the community of Muhammad Ali, he has focused this work on preserving the stories of pioneering Muslim Americans, investing in youth development and replicating models that work.

His efforts have contributed to recovery and reentry services, hundreds of units of affordable housing placed in service, and jobs for Americans too often left out.

  • BA, Government, Politics and African American StudiesUniversity of Maryland
  • Diploma, Arabic and Islamic JurisprudenceAbu Nour, Syria
